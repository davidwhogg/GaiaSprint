2026 Gaia Relay: Stanford Satellite, Leg 1

During Leg 1 of the 2026 Gaia Relay, the Kavli Institute for Particle Astrophysics and Cosmology (KIPAC) at Stanford University will host a satellite relay event on Dec 2-4.

Location

KIPAC, Stanford University, Stanford, CA

Apply to participate

If you'd like to attend, please contact Ani Chiti (achiti@stanford.edu) or Ioana Ciuca (iciuca@stanford.edu) by October 31st. In-person space is limited to 50 participants, and local attendees will be prioritized.
