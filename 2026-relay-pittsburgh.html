2026 Gaia Relay: Pittsburgh Satellite, Leg 1

During Leg 1 of the 2026 Gaia Relay, Carnegie Mellon University and the University of Pittsburgh will jointly host a satellite relay event on Dec 2-4.

Location

Carnegie Mellon University and the University of Pittsburgh, Pittsburgh, PA. Room details will be sent to registrants.

  • December 2nd -- CMU, Wean Hall
  • December 3rd -- CMU, Wean Hall
  • December 4th -- UPitt, Allen Hall
