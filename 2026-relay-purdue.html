2026 Gaia Relay: Purdue Satellite, Leg 1

For Leg 1 of the 2026 Gaia Relay (Dec 1-4), Purdue University will host a satellite relay event.

Location

Purdue University, West Lafayette, IN

Apply to participate

Please fill out the form below by Nov. 9 to indicate whether you would like to attend virtually or in person to the satellite event. Sign Up Form

Meeting Schedule

Tuesday, Dec. 1 - 1:30 to 3:30 Room 398
Wednesday, Dec. 2 - 1:30 to 3:30 Room 350
Thursday, Dec. 3 - 1:30 to 5:00 Room 398
Friday, Dec. 4 - 9:00 to 10:00 Room 398
All meetings will be in the Physics Main Building. Link to join virtually via Teams will be available soon.

Session Information

Tuesday will be a brainstorming session to discuss plans/ideas for what to do with the data.
Wednesday (release day!) and Thursday will be "hack-a-thon" style sessions with Gaia DR4.
Friday will start with a "tag-up" with the NYC leg of the relay to update on what worked/didn't work
