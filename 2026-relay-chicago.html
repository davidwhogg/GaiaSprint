2026 Gaia Relay: Chicago Satellite, Leg 3

For Leg 3 of the 2026 Gaia Relay (Dec 14-17), the NSF-Simons SkAI institute, in conjunction with the Kavli Institute for Cosmological Physics (KICP) and the Center for Interdisciplinary Exploration and Research in Astrophysics (CIERA), will host a satellite relay event.

Apply to participate (due Oct 30)

Registration link: Link to registration form

Logistics

Location: NSF-Simons SkAI Hub: 875 N. Michigan Ave., Suite 3500, Chicago, IL 60611

We will be limited to 50 participants. Information for hotel blocks will be provided soon.

We will run the event and provide coffee and lunch for the first four days of the week. On Dec 18, the SkAI space will be available to those interested in continuing for another day, but we will not have any formal events or food.
